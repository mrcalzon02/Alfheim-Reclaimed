# The Twin Spines

**Status:** design record. Interlock rules fixed; recipe implementation not yet written.
**Reads with:** `CAMPAIGN_ERAS.md` for the era-by-era track content.

---

## 1. Why two spines and not two options

A pack with two magic mods usually lets the player pick one and ignore the other. That produces half
a pack twice. Here they are **twins**: two halves of one tradition that was broken by the devastation,
and neither half works properly alone.

| | Spine of Leaf | Spine of Song |
|---|---|---|
| Mods | Botania, MythicBotany | Ars Nouveau |
| Resource | Mana | Source |
| Generated by | Flowers consuming the world | Sourcelinks consuming activity |
| Produces | Materials, metals, power | Effects, knowledge, automation |
| Fails alone at | Automation and utility | Ever making anything permanent |
| Elven metaphor | The body of the world | The voice that shapes it |

Leaf makes things. Song makes things *happen*. An elf with only Leaf has a garden that grows metal
and no way to work it at scale. An elf with only Song has enormous capability and nothing to spend it
on.

## 2. The interlock

Four hard dependencies run between the spines. These are the mechanical claim that the twins are real
and not thematic dressing. Each is implemented as a recipe or ritual requirement, not merely a quest
prerequisite.

### 2.1 Leaf → Song: Source needs living material

Ars Nouveau's Sourcelinks are re-specified to require Botania output:

- **Agronomic Sourcelink** (Era II) requires Livingrock in place of vanilla stone. Source generation
  is therefore gated behind the player's first working mana infrastructure.
- **Alchemical** and **Volcanic Sourcelinks** (Era V) require Elementium components.

Consequence: you cannot bootstrap Song before Leaf. Era I gives Song only a spell book and a notebook
for exactly this reason.

### 2.2 Song → Leaf: Mana infusion needs ritual

The high-mana infusions are re-gated behind Ars Nouveau ritual work:

- **Alfsteel Ingot** (1,500,000 mana) requires a Ritual Brazier active within the Mana Infuser's
  structure — a Song ritual is part of the Leaf recipe.
- **Terrasteel** (500,000 mana) likewise, in Era X.

Consequence: the two pinnacle metals of the pack are literally co-authored. Neither spine can claim
them.

### 2.3 Shared scarcity: Archwood and Dreamwood

Ars Nouveau's four Archwood species and Botania's Dreamwood are both magical woods. They are placed
in the same forests in Alfheim, so gathering for one spine feeds the other, and clearing land for a
mana farm costs you Song material. This is the ordinary, moment-to-moment version of the interlock —
the one the player feels without reading a quest.

### 2.4 The gate needs both

The Alfheim Portal (Era IV) is a Leaf structure. Under the reversal it must be *attuned* before it
will trade, and attunement is a Song ritual using the Scry glyphs. The pack's pivotal moment requires
both spines at once, by construction.

## 3. Pacing

Both tracks run 7 quests per era from Era II onward. They are not required to be balanced *within* an
era — one may clearly lead — but the era capstone requires both complete.

| Era | Leaf leads on | Song leads on |
|---|---|---|
| I–II | Everything | Nothing; Song is being discovered |
| III | Mana automation | Familiars and Sourcelinks catch up |
| IV | The gate | Attunement makes the gate work |
| V | Metals | Enchanting and armour |
| VI–VIII | Capacity and tiers | Knowledge, glyph library, research |
| IX | Ritual materials | Death-aspect ritual |
| X | Terrasteel, Mjöllnir | The autonomous source economy |

Song deliberately starts weak and ends as the more powerful of the two. Leaf front-loads. An elf
rebuilds the body of the world first and remembers how to sing to it afterwards — that is the
narrative reason, and it also gives the pack a difficulty curve that does not flatten in the
late game.

## 4. What supports what

Everything outside the twins is support, and each support mod is assigned to exactly one spine or to
the world itself. A support mod with no assignment is a candidate for removal.

| Support | Serves | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer's Delight, The Harvest, Miner's Delight | Leaf | Horticulture as the base of the food economy |
| MineColonies + Structurize + Domum Ornamentum | World | Settlement restoration; the visible progress bar |
| Conquest Reforged, FramedBlocks, Supplementaries, Macaw's | World | Elven architecture |
| Twilight Forest, BetterEnd, Regions Unexplored | World | Expedition ground |
| Occultism | Song | Era IX cross-binding only; not a third spine |
| Modonomicon, Patchouli | Both | In-world documentation |
| Mine and Slash + Library of Exile | The Wound | Reward economy — see `INSTRUCTIONS.md` §2.2 |
| Create family (12 mods) | **Unassigned** | See `CAMPAIGN_ERAS.md` §4.2 |

## 5. Validation

The interlock is only real if it is implemented. Each rule in §2 is a recipe change, and each must
reach level 11 (a player completing it in-game) before the spine design is anything but `draft`.

The specific risk to watch: §2.1 and §2.2 create a **circular-looking** dependency — Song needs Leaf's
Livingrock, Leaf's Alfsteel needs Song's brazier. It is not actually circular, because Livingrock is
Era II and Alfsteel is Era V. But it is close enough that the era ordering must be verified in-game
before either change is committed, or the pack can deadlock in a way no static check will catch.
