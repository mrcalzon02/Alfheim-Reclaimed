# Progression Blueprint

## I — The Withered Grove
Survive among elven ruins. Establish mundane horticulture and recover the first fragments of botanical knowledge.

## II — Livingwood
Restore a Pure Daisy and begin producing Livingwood and Livingrock. Botania becomes the technological foundation.

## III — The First Garden
Build functional mana gardens. Early automation is botanical rather than mechanical.

## IV — Restoration
Repair ruined settlements, gardens, homes, workshops and shrines. Exploration becomes a source of progression unlocks.

## V — The Lost Arts
Recover Ars Nouveau and Nature's Aura knowledge from academies, libraries and damaged ecological infrastructure.

## VI — The Courts
Encounter Feywild factions and surviving fey institutions. Their objectives do not necessarily agree.

## VII — Terrasteel
Re-establish enough magical industry to manufacture high-tier materials and restore major civic infrastructure.

## VIII — The Alfheim Gate
Use MythicBotany progression to reopen the route to Alfheim.

## IX — Alfheim Reclaimed
Discover that Alfheim is itself ruined. The late game becomes restoration of the elven homeland rather than merely reaching it.
