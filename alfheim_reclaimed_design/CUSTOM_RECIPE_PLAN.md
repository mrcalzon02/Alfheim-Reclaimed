# Custom Recipe Integration Plan

This file is a design target, not an active script.

## Design doctrine
1. Botania is elven technology.
2. Ars Nouveau is elven sorcery.
3. Nature's Aura represents environmental health and restoration.
4. Feywild supplies surviving peoples/factions rather than disconnected fantasy mobs.
5. Farmer's Delight supports horticultural settlement life.
6. Vanilla technological shortcuts should eventually be replaced only where the replacement is purposeful.
7. Important unlocks should be tied to recovered structures, quest milestones, or restored ecological systems.

## Early recipe candidates
- Rework initial Botania access so the player recovers botanical knowledge before full progression.
- Integrate Livingwood/Livingrock into repaired elven workstations and Ars Nouveau components.
- Use Sourceberries, petals, herbs and Farmer's Delight crops as ingredients in elven consumables.
- Make selected Nature's Aura components depend on restored botanical infrastructure.
- Gate Alfheim access behind a multi-settlement restoration sequence rather than a single crafting recipe.

## Safety rule for implementation
Change one progression family at a time and verify JEI, recipe availability and quest completion before
removing the previous route. Do not apply a pack-wide recipe purge before replacement recipes exist.
