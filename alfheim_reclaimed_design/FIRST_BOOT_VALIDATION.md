# First Boot Validation

1. Import the ZIP into CurseForge as a custom profile.
2. Confirm Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.10 is selected.
3. Launch once without adding other mods.
4. Reach the title screen.
5. Create a disposable test world.
6. Confirm JEI and Jade are visible.
7. Confirm Botania, MythicBotany, Ars Nouveau, Nature's Aura and Feywild content appears in JEI.
8. Open the FTB Quests interface and confirm it loads.
9. Confirm KubeJS reports the Alfheim Reclaimed baseline script in the log.
10. Only after this baseline passes should recipe removal, quest SNBT, custom structures or performance mods be layered in.
