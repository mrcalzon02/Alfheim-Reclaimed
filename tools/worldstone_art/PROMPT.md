Create one square, seamless, opaque tileable Minecraft block texture for a gigantic crimson Worldstone crystal. This is a production material texture, straight-on orthographic flat surface, edge-to-edge material, no background, no text, no border, no single isolated gemstone. Deep blood-red translucent-looking mineral facets with sharp angular planar fractures, dark garnet-red broad planes, thin luminous scarlet veins and a few ruby highlights. Clearly hand-crafted pixel-art texture suitable for downsampling to 32x32 pixels. Fine facets distributed evenly, no central circular glow, no smooth photographic noise, no brick mortar, no actual transparency. The final tile must repeat cleanly in both directions, with matching colors and forms along opposite edges. Restrained rich red palette, avoid pink, orange, purple, blue and white.
